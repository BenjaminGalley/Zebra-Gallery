Market research · reference examples

Know the market. Value each piece.

Hemingway African Gallery's current online listings provide useful international retail comparisons for Ethiopian chairs and tables. These are asking prices for its individual pieces—not Zebra Gallery prices.

Welega chairs · Ethiopia

$960–$1,500

Observed listed prices for selected examples. Hemingway describes the traditional form as carved from a single block of wood, with a deep patina on its antique examples.

Jimma chairs · Ethiopia

$1,500–$3,500

Observed listed prices across selected examples, including painted sculptural chairs and an antique example. Hemingway links the style to household furniture associated with King Abba Jifar.

Tables · Ethiopia

$450–$800

Hemingway listings include a small antique Ingera table at $450 and colorful or round side tables at $800. These are different item types and should not be grouped as stools.
