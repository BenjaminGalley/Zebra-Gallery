## Pricing rules for Zebra Gallery

1. For a directly comparable item, calculate an initial target at 70% of the competitor's currently displayed asking price (30% below). Prefer the live product page over an old collection-page snippet.
2. If a competitor has a temporary sale, record both the regular and sale price. Do not silently treat a sale as its normal price.
3. Match only like-for-like: regional form, scale, material, age, condition, paint/patina, carving quality and provenance.
4. For stools, use Ethiopian stool comparisons first. Do not label Tonga (Zimbabwe), Asante (Ghana), or Bamileke (Cameroon) stools as Ethiopian. Use them only as clearly identified wider African-market context.
5. The 30%-below figure is a positioning target, not a promise that the gallery can profit at that price. Before approving, subtract acquisition cost, restoration, packing, export documentation, payment fees, taxes, shipping subsidy and desired margin.
6. Final prices must be approved by the gallery owner and tied to a Zebra item/SKU. Until a physical Zebra item is matched and measured, show “Enquire for price” rather than attaching another seller's price to it.
7. Display USD as the international reference currency. If local ETB pricing is later added, calculate it from the exchange rate used on the date of quotation and state that date; do not hard-code a stale conversion.
8. No competitor source reviewed here provides reliable completed transaction prices or proof of sale volume. These are public asking prices only.

## Website presentation
- Product cards: original Zebra image, product name, region/style where known, one-sentence description, exact measurements, condition, USD price only after owner approval, availability, enquiry button.
- Product detail: dimensions in cm and inches; specify width, depth, total height and seat height separately for chairs/stools where applicable.
- Do not copy competitor photography, full descriptions, or imply their product is Zebra stock.
- Never invent age, wood species, provenance, maker, condition, measurements or availability.
- For an unmeasured or not-yet-matched Zebra item, show “Measurements being confirmed” and “Enquire for price”; do not use placeholder stock photos.
